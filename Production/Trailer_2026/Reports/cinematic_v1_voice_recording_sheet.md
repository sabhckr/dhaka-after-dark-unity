## Delivery

48 kHz, 24-bit mono WAV, one file per line, named `line_NN_CHARACTER.wav`. Record dry, in a quiet room, 20 cm from the microphone. The mouths in the picture follow each written line (Elara and Vance through their own jaw bones, Leo and Marcus through a jaw deformation in the trailer shader), not a recording, so a real take will not match them syllable for syllable. Timing only needs to land inside each window.
