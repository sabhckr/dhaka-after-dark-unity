============================================================
PART 1 — PURPOSE AND PRODUCTION ORDER
============================================================

This directive establishes the geometric and functional detailing
standard for DHAKA: AFTER DARK.

The objective is to build a believable, physically detailed,
recognisable Dhaka environment.

The city must not look like a collection of generic 3D buildings,
roads and props.

Every major object should appear constructed, assembled,
installed, maintained, repaired, aged and used in the real world.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION:

A. GEOMETRIC DETAILING
Actual physical geometry and components.

B. SURFACE DETAILING
Material properties, textures, scratches, rust, stains,
dirt, weathering, fabric and surface imperfections.

C. FUNCTIONAL DETAILING
Components that move, operate, illuminate or interact.

These are separate requirements.

Do not substitute textures for important physical geometry.

Do not use excessive polygons where a normal map or texture
is sufficient.

Use physical geometry for:
- Silhouettes.
- Structural components.
- Components visible at close range.
- Objects casting meaningful shadows.
- Moving or interactive parts.
- Components whose depth is visually important.

Use textures and normal maps for:
- Fine surface imperfections.
- Small lettering.
- Fabric patterns.
- Fine scratches.
- Minor surface roughness.
- Small bolts and surface markings where geometry is unnecessary.
