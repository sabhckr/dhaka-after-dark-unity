# Detailing standards

Milestone 1 of the owner's detailing directive ([DETAILING_DIRECTIVE.md](DETAILING_DIRECTIVE.md), 2026-11-03): how this project turns the directive into rules, names, inventory, references and levels of detail. Written after D-05, from an inspection of the project as it stands at commit c69c9d6.

The directive is the long-term standard. This document is how it is applied here; where the two differ, the reason is given (section 9). The requirement-by-requirement status is in [../ASSET_INVENTORY](../ASSET_INVENTORY/README.md).

## 1. What the project is today

**How the world is built**

- Everything in the world is **generated in code from the map data**, on the streaming workers, one mesh per kind per 500 m chunk: ground, roads (with markings, kerbs, sidewalks, stop pads and drains), buildings, debug lines.
- Surfaces use **one shader** (`Dad/World`) and **16 generated texture layers** (D-02; 16.3 MB of GPU memory). The material id is carried in each vertex.
- **No modelled asset exists yet.** No mesh comes from Blender or a library, and nothing has been downloaded except the Bengali fonts in `Art/Fonts` (approved).
- **Blender is not installed** on the development machine. Installing it is a download and needs the owner's approval.

**What the map data offers in the Farmgate slice** (12 chunks)

| Data | Count in the slice | Used by a builder today |
|---|---|---|
| Roads, with class, width (mostly estimated), lanes, sidewalk, surface, lit | 554 | Yes, except `surface` and `lit` |
| Buildings, with footprint and height (97.5 % estimated) | 5,888; 431 with a type (126 commercial, 110 apartments, 95 houses, 8 mosques, 8 hospitals…), 5 with a material, 5 with a roof shape | Footprint, height and type for heights; not material or roof |
| Trees | 199 (109 mapped, 90 approximate); no species | No |
| Street lamps | 64 | No |
| Bus stops, crossings, signals, gates | 13, 17, 4, 15 | Stops, crossings and signals; not gates |
| Places of worship, hospitals, government, hotels, universities, stations (landmarks) | 20, 19, 7, 5, 3, 2 | No (names only) |
| Rails (metro alignment) | 40 pieces | No: the Line 6 viaduct and track are not drawn |
| Barriers (walls, fences) | 18 | No |
| Power lines | **0** | — |
| Footbridge ways | some (5 in c_7_-30) | Left out by the road builder |

So poles and overhead cables have no data in the slice and need a placement rule; trees and lamps have positions but no species or type.

## 2. Three kinds of detail

The directive separates geometric, surface and functional detail. Each requirement in the inventory says which it needs.

**Geometry** is used when one of these holds:

- it changes the **silhouette** seen from the street (poles, railings, balconies, canopies, tanks);
- it has **depth of 3 cm or more that is seen within 10 m** (kerbs 15 cm, drain lips 6 cm, sunken slabs 4 cm, window reveals, shutters);
- it casts a **shadow that matters inside the 150 m shadow distance** (anything 0.3 m or more across that stands off a surface);
- it is **walked on, driven into, collided with or moved** (steps, ramps, bollards, doors, wheels, gates);
- it is part of a **structure that must read as engineered** (piers, beams, crossarms, bearings).

**Textures and normal maps** carry everything finer: cracks, joints under 1 cm, scratches, stains, small lettering, fabric weave, small bolts, fine roughness. They come from the generated layers (D-02) or, for kits, from shared atlases.

**Function** is anything that moves, lights, operates or responds. A functional item is only marked done when it works in play and a test or a recorded check shows it. A visual stand-in is recorded as such ("visual only"), never as functional.

## 3. Two routes to an asset

| Route | For | How | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Built from data in code** | Things that follow the map: roads, kerbs, drains, dividers, facades on footprints, viaducts on the rail alignment, cables between poles | A builder in `Dad.World` (worker thread, no Unity dependency), part of the chunk meshes, tested in EditMode against the data | From the map; a labelled rule where the map is silent (like K-30, K-34) |
| **B. Modular kit** | Discrete objects: poles, transformers, street lights, stalls, shop fittings, signs, tanks, vehicles, people | Modelled (Blender, once approved) or generated by an editor tool in C#, as the textures are; imported as meshes with LODs; drawn instanced | Placed per chunk by a rule or from data points (lamps, trees, stops), on the worker; instanced on the main thread |

Route B needs a decision before Milestone 2: **how instanced kit objects are drawn and streamed** (per-chunk instance lists drawn with `Graphics.RenderMeshInstanced` or a `BatchRendererGroup`, against one GameObject per prop). It touches streaming and the 4 ms apply rule, so it will be proposed with measurements, not assumed.

## 4. Naming

**Inventory ids:** `<FILE>-<SUB>-<NNN>`, for example `RD-MRK-004` (Roads, markings, item 4). Ids are never reused; a dropped requirement keeps its row with a note.

| File code | File | File code | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| RD | Roads.csv | VH | Vehicles.csv |
| FP | Footpaths.csv | CH | Characters.csv |
| UT | Utilities.csv (power, telecom, covers, drainage, street lights) | SH | Shops.csv |
| BD | Buildings.csv | VG | Vegetation.csv |
| MT | Metro.csv | PS | PublicSpaces.csv |
| FB | Footbridges.csv | TR | Transport.csv |
| RL | ReligiousArchitecture.csv | SG | Signage.csv |
| CS | Construction.csv | MA | Materials.csv (weathering and surface conditions) |

**Assets:** `<Domain>_<Family>_<Component>_<Variant>[_LOD<n>]`, CamelCase parts, ASCII only. The domain is the file code.

- `UT_PoleConcrete_Crossarm_A_LOD0`, `SH_TeaStall_Kettle_B`, `VH_Cng_Canopy_A_LOD1`, `MT_Viaduct_PierCap_A`.
- Materials: `<Domain>_<Family>_<Surface>`, for example `UT_PoleConcrete_Concrete`.
- Atlases: `<Domain>_<Family>_Atlas_<Albedo|Normal|Mask>`.

**Folders:**

- Sources (`.blend`, reference notes) in `Art/<Domain>/<Family>/`, beside `Art/Fonts`, each family with a `LICENSES.md`.
- Unity assets in `Assets/_Project/Art/<Domain>/<Family>/{Meshes,Materials,Textures,Prefabs}`.
- Code stays in its assemblies: data-driven builders in `Dad.World` (`<Thing>Builder.cs`, rules in `<Thing>Rules`), kit placement beside them.

## 5. Inventory

The inventory is in [`Docs/ASSET_INVENTORY`](../ASSET_INVENTORY/README.md), one CSV per file code above. The directive's suggested `Documentation/` tree is kept under the existing `Docs/` instead, so there is one documentation tree, not two.

**Columns:** `ID, Category, Subcategory, Description, Geometry, Texture, Functional, Priority, Reusability, LOD, Status, Reference, Validation, Directive`. The last column says which part of the directive the row comes from.

**Deduplication.** A requirement that the directive lists in more than one place (manholes in parts 3 and 6; benches in 5 and 19; shelters in 5, 19 and 20; tree guards in 5 and 18; dust in 3 and 22) has one row. The row lists every part it answers in `Directive`. Per-family items (tyres on a sedan, a CNG and a coach) are separate requirements and keep separate rows.

**Statuses** and what each needs:

| Status | Needs |
|---|---|
| NOT STARTED | Nothing exists, or only a placeholder (say which in `Validation`) |
| RESEARCHED | References gathered and recorded, design rules written; nothing built |
| BLOCKED | Cannot start; the reason is in `Validation` (missing data, an approval, a dependency) |
| IN PROGRESS | Partly built: some of what the row asks exists in the build, and the rest is named |
| IMPLEMENTED | Built in the Unity project and seen in a Unity shot; no automated test yet |
| TESTED | Implemented, with an automated test or golden view that fails if it breaks (named in `Validation`) |
| APPROVED | Tested and accepted by the owner. Only the owner sets this |

A grey box, a colour or a debug marker is a placeholder: NOT STARTED, with the placeholder named.

**Priority:** P1 is the directive's Milestone 2 Farmgate selection; P2 Milestones 3–5 within the slice; P3 Milestone 6 and items that depend on later phases (night lights, rain, gameplay); P4 expansion, airport and interiors with no gameplay yet.

**Check:** `Tools/inventory-check.py` (CLT python) checks columns, unique ids, the id pattern, allowed statuses and priorities, duplicate descriptions within a subcategory, and validation notes on every status above NOT STARTED, and prints counts by status. Run it after every edit.

## 6. Levels of detail

The tiers follow the directive (Part 25), set against what the project already has: a 60° camera at 1280×720 on the M1, a 150 m shadow distance, and chunks of 500 m loaded two rings out.

| Tier | Distance | What is drawn | Shadows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Player, story characters, player vehicle, key landmarks at any distance | LOD0 always within 50 m, own textures allowed | Yes |
| Near | 0–30 m | LOD0: full geometry from section 2 | Yes |
| Mid | 30–150 m | LOD1: about a quarter of LOD0's triangles; small parts (bolts, handles, cable clamps) dropped; shared materials | Yes, to 150 m |
| Distant | 150 m to the load ring | LOD2 or an impostor; props under 1 m dropped; cables, laundry, fittings dropped | No |
| Beyond | Outside the load rings | Nothing | — |

**Targets (labelled targets, not measurements; each kit is measured in the bench before it is accepted):**

| Kind | LOD0 triangles | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Street prop (bollard, bin, sign) | ≤ 1,500 | Shared atlas, no own set |
| Pole with crossarms and fittings | ≤ 4,000 | Shared atlas |
| Stall or shopfront kit | ≤ 15,000 | Shared atlas per family |
| Tree | ≤ 20,000, with 2 LODs and an impostor | One set per species |
| Vehicle (traffic) | ≤ 40,000 | One set per model |
| Vehicle (player) | ≤ 120,000, interior included | One set per model, 2K at most |
| Person (crowd / near / hero) | 4,000 / 15,000 / 60,000 | Shared / per outfit / own |

**Frame budget.** The street bench draws 423–436 thousand triangles at a GPU p50 of 7.9–8.4 ms on the M1 at 720p (D-05). The HIGH-tier targets (3 M triangles, 2,000 batches, 12 ms GPU at 1440p) are for Windows hardware that has not been measured (K-02). Each Milestone 2 item reports its own triangles, batches and GPU change on the street path.

**Texel density targets:** 512 px a metre for hero objects, 256 for near props, 128 for building facades. A 2K or 4K set for a small roadside object is not used.

**Culling and pooling:** frustum culling as now; occlusion culling is to be measured in the dense blocks before it is adopted; kit objects instanced and pooled per chunk, never one GameObject per prop.

## 7. References and licensing

- **Provenance is recorded per row** (`Reference`) and per art family (`Art/<Domain>/<Family>/LICENSES.md`): who made the source, where it came from, its licence, the date.
- **Observation references** (photos, drawings, documents viewed online) are recorded by title, author or site and address. They are not stored in the repository unless their licence allows it and the owner approves the download. This is how the owner's character and vehicle brief is kept (images hotlinked, read as text).
- **Allowed sources for assets**, each needing approval before download: the project's own generators; original modelling; CC0 or CC-BY libraries (Poly Haven, ambientCG and similar), with the licence recorded. Not allowed: anything ripped from games (GTA, BUSSID or others), commercial models without a purchase the owner approves, and any image or model whose licence is unknown.
- **Names and brands:** businesses and products are fictional and original. The real coach and airline names already in the project stay, as the owner decided; no new trademark is added without approval.
- **Bengali text** is real Bengali, written and checked, shaped correctly (conjuncts, vowel signs). Shaping is a D-09 risk: Unity's text shaping for Bengali must be checked before any sign is accepted. Never generated pseudo-Bengali.
- **Places of worship and the metro** are built from real references of Dhaka buildings and the real Line 6 system, recorded per row. No sacred feature is invented, and traditions are never mixed into one generic building.
- **Dhaka standards to source:** the BRTA Traffic Signs Manual (K-29), Dhaka Metro Rail (DMTCL) station and viaduct references, DESCO and DPDC pole and transformer practice. Until one is sourced, the rows that depend on it stay below RESEARCHED.

## 8. Acceptance

Part 30's fourteen checks, and how this project shows each:

| Check | Evidence here |
|---|---|
| 1–2 Geometry inspected; components present | A close golden view per kit or feature, looked at side by side; for data-driven geometry, an EditMode test of the parts (as `SidewalkTests` checks closed edges) |
| 3–5 Materials, UVs, normals | The F5 shading views (normals, material ids, UVs) in a Unity shot; an import check of UV range and texel density for kits |
| 6 Scale | Dimensions in the inventory row, checked in Unity against a reference measure |
| 7–8 URP and world architecture | It streams in and out with the chunks; EditMode and PlayMode suites pass |
| 9 LOD | Shots at 10, 50 and 200 m; triangle counts per LOD in the inventory |
| 10 Performance | `Tools/bench.sh` on the air and street paths, before and after; figures in PERFORMANCE_REPORT |
| 11 Real environment | In the Farmgate slice at noon and at night (`tod-*` views) |
| 12–13 Documentation, references | The inventory row, the art family's `LICENSES.md` |
| 14 Nothing broken | Full regression, goldens re-checked, as for every milestone |

Screenshots are Unity shots (`Tools/shot.sh`); a Blender viewport is never evidence. Figures are measured or not given.

## 9. How the directive fits the plan

**Milestones.** The directive's milestones map onto the existing roadmap like this:

| Directive | Existing plan | Note |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Detailing standard | This document and the inventory (2026-11-03) | — |
| M2 Farmgate validation | Pieces of D-06 (one facade), D-07 (street trees), D-08 (pole, cables, street light), D-10 (one shop), D-11 (one divider, one footbridge), plus a viaduct section | One representative of each, validated together, instead of finishing D-06 to D-11 in order |
| M3 Transport detailing | Metro: new to Phase D. Vehicles: Phase E (E-04 to E-06) | The owner said on 2026-11-02 not to start characters or vehicles in Phase D |
| M4 Architectural detailing | D-06, rest of D-09 and D-10 | — |
| M5 City infrastructure | D-08, D-11, D-12, D-13 | — |
| M6 Character detailing | Phase E (E-01, E-02) and F | — |
| M7 World expansion | D-14 "then scale", Phase I | — |

**Where the directive and the project differ**

1. **Blender.** The directive names Blender for modelling. It is not installed. Everything so far is generated in code, which suits data-following geometry (route A) and keeps the storage small. Kits (route B) can start either way; Blender needs the owner's approval to install, and its disk use counts against the 25 GB budget.
2. **Character and vehicle priority.** The directive says the earlier character and vehicle directive takes priority. That brief was kept for Phases E and F, and the owner said then not to start that work during Phase D. This standard keeps that order until the owner says otherwise.
3. **Road widths.** The directive says not to invent widths where the data has them. In the slice most widths are estimated by the exporter because OSM has none; the estimate is labelled (K-31), and overlapping twins are a known issue.
4. **No elevation.** The world is flat (K-05). Water that follows terrain, flood-prone dips and ramps to bridges wait on an elevation source, which needs the owner's approval.
5. **Power lines.** The slice has no mapped power lines. Poles and cables will be placed by a labelled rule along streets, with the 64 mapped lamps where they stand.
6. **Folder name.** The directive suggests `Documentation/`; the project keeps `Docs/` (one tree).
