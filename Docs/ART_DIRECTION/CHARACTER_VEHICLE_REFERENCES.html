DHAKA: AFTER DARK

Character & Vehicle Art Direction
Grounded urban realism inspired by the atmosphere and character work of GTA IV, modern character-production techniques, and the detailed vehicle modelling philosophy associated with BUSSID — interpreted as an original Dhaka game.

Reference-use note: These images are visual references, not assets to copy or import. Use them to understand modelling detail, material response, topology, texture workflow and presentation. Create original models and obtain appropriate licences for any production assets.

01 · Character appearance and world feel

Aim for believable everyday people, expressive faces, natural clothing and grounded city-life presentation. The GTA IV reference is about mood and lived-in character presence, not copying its characters.

02 · Modelling, topology and UVs

Game-ready assets need clean deformation topology and deliberate UV layouts. High-poly detail should be transferred efficiently rather than carried into every runtime mesh.

03 · Vehicle exterior and local vehicle identity

Vehicles should have convincing proportions, distinct silhouettes, functional-looking components and locally appropriate design. Build original assets using photographic references.

04 · Materials and Unity integration

The final appearance must be validated in the project's actual Unity 6 URP scene. Blender viewport quality alone is not acceptance.

  • Skin: base colour, normal, roughness and a restrained URP-compatible skin response.
  • Fabric: cotton, denim, knitwear, uniform fabric and realistic folds.
  • Vehicle paint: controlled reflections, plausible roughness and subtle wear.
  • Rubber: tyre sidewalls, tread detail and high roughness.
  • Glass: correct transparency and reflections without mirror-like windows.
  • Metal and plastic: separate material response for trim, grilles, dashboard and fittings.
  • Performance: LODs, texture atlases, shared materials, culling and measured frame time.

05 · First production slice

Prove the pipeline with a small set before expanding the asset library.

Project constraints: Unity 6 + URP · M1 MacBook Air, 8 GB unified memory · 25 GB project storage budget. Preserve existing world data, chunk streaming and completed engineering systems. No external downloads or purchases without approval.
