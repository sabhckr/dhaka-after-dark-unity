# D-06c: near facades

The third part of D-06 as re-scoped by the owner on 2026-11-03 ([ENVIRONMENT_ROADMAP.md](ENVIRONMENT_ROADMAP.md), after [D06A_CLASSIFICATION_FAR_TIER.md](D06A_CLASSIFICATION_FAR_TIER.md) and [D06B_ROOFS_MATERIALS.md](D06B_ROOFS_MATERIALS.md)): within about 150 m, the walls the far tier paints get their relief in geometry, in eight near styles, and fade in and out by level of detail without popping. Footprints, heights, roads, rails, piers, chunk boundaries and the far tier are unchanged (byte for byte, below); no texture or asset was added or downloaded. Level 1 landmarks: a candidate list only, for the owner to review ([D06C/L1_LANDMARK_CANDIDATES.md](D06C/L1_LANDMARK_CANDIDATES.md)); no landmark geometry.

## What is drawn (`Runtime/World/NearFacades.cs`)

Each wall the far tier paints (D-06a: the outer ring of a building on the ground, 2.5 m or longer, not cut along the chunk border, not one of Milestone 2's 76 validation-area buildings) keeps its bays and floors. The near tier adds pieces that stand proud of the wall on the same bays and floors, at the edges of the windows the shader paints, and skips the windows the shader bricks up: the shader's cell hash is ported to C# (`CellHash`), and a test reads the shader to keep the two in step. The wall itself, its painted glass, shutters and signs are not touched; nothing is cut into a wall.

**Styles.** Every style is a **labelled inference**: no facade is mapped in the data, and no photo reference was used. A building's style comes from its D-06a category and type and, for homes and shop-houses, its district:

| Style | Which buildings | Pieces |
|---|---|---|
| WalkUp | Residential and other, outside the two district lists (the residential default; the roadmap's dense and Mohammadpur-style walk-up apartments) | A sill and a cast sunshade (chhajja) at every window; a steel box grille where the window has room; on walls facing a road, a balcony with a solid parapet on about a third of the upper bays |
| ShopHouse | Mixed use | On the ground floor of a road-facing wall, shops: pilasters between them, the rolling shutter's box, a signboard over the painted one (blank), an awning on about half; the district's homes above |
| Office | Commercial, not a market | Mullions at the bay lines, a spandrel between floors (both in the wall's material); on a road-facing wall a signboard (blank) and a canopy over the glazed shopfronts |
| Market | Commercial typed retail, shop, supermarket, mall or market, or classed commercial for its size | Shopfronts as shop-houses have, under the painted rolled-up shutters; a continuous sunshade and sill band on each upper floor |
| Industrial | Industrial | Pilasters at the bay lines, a hood over each strip window; on a road-facing wall a closed roller shutter in about half the ground-floor bays |
| Institutional | Institutional | A frame of columns and floor beams, a sunshade band at the window heads, a sill at each window |
| OldDhaka | Homes whose nearest named place is in the Old Dhaka list | Open shutter leaves in painted wood beside each window, a hood moulding over it, a cornice at each floor line, small balconies with iron railings |
| Modern | Homes whose nearest named place is in the modern list | Slab edges at each floor line; on a road-facing wall, balconies with glass railings on about half the upper bays; fins at every other bay line |

**Districts** (`Districts`), also an inference: the data has no district boundaries, so a building takes the district of the nearest named place within 1,500 m (`places.json`, 732 places). Old Dhaka is 52 quarters of the old city between the Buriganga and Fulbaria (Babu Bazar, Islampur, Shakhari Bazar, Armanitola, Chawkbazar, Lalbagh, Wari, Gendaria and others, listed in the code); Modern is Gulshan, Gulshan 1 and 2, Banani, Baridhara, Baridhara DOHS, Dhanmondi and Mirpur DOHS (9 places match).

**Against the roadmap's list.** The original D-06 row names nine families; this milestone draws eight near styles. Mohammadpur-style walk-ups and the dense residential default are one style (WalkUp), and Dhanmondi residences share the Modern style with Gulshan and Banani; the roadmap's D-06c row asks for eight. Telling Dhanmondi from Gulshan, or Mohammadpur from other walk-ups, needs references the project does not have.

**Room in front.** Each bay looks straight out from its centre for the nearest footprint edge in the chunk (any building, its own included), up to 12 m, and to the chunk border plus 1 m (what stands beyond is not known). A bay with less than 1 m gets nothing (party walls, close neighbours); elsewhere nothing reaches further than the room less 0.4 m, and a balcony needs its whole depth. Slice bays: under 1 m 4.4 %, 1–2 m 10.7 %, 2–3 m 12.3 %, 3–6 m 21.3 %, 6–12 m 17.5 %, 12 m or more 33.8 %. In Old Dhaka's dense lanes (c_8_-21) 32.9 % of bays have under 1 m and stay plain.

**Geometry budget.** The pieces are open boxes with no back face (a sill 6 triangles, a sunshade 10, a box grille 12: plate, two posts and a rail; no bars, which would sparkle beyond about 20 m). An early version without these savings drew 12.9 M triangles in the slice; this one 6.78 M.

## Levels of detail without popping

Each street cell's near facades are built on a worker with its near rooftop kit (K-45 approach 4: when the cell comes within 250 m, dropped beyond 350 m) and uploaded in the same mesh: submesh 0 the rooftop kit in `World.mat`, submesh 1 the near facades in `WorldNear.mat` (the same shader and settings, with the keyword `_DAD_NEAR`). They are shown and hidden with the cell's near tier, as the kit is.

In all four passes (lit, shadow, depth, depth-normals) the shader dissolves each pixel of the near facades in by a 4 × 4 ordered (screen-door) dither as its distance from the focus goes from **140 m** (none drawn) to **110 m** (all drawn). The distance is measured as `StreetLod` measures the cells' switch: on the ground plan, plus the focus's height above 10 m. Every piece belongs to the cell of its bay's centre and lies within 7.7 m of it, and the cell switches at 150 m, so 140 + 7.7 < 150: **no near facade is drawn at the moment its cell switches**, and none appears or vanishes at once. The streamer sets the focus and the fade each frame (`_DadNearFocus`, `_DadNearFade`); unset, everything is drawn.

The alternative, sliding pieces out of the wall (a geomorph), was not used: a glass railing or a shutter leaf crossing the wall face shows its whole front at once, a pop in colour.

**Colour through the fade.** The golden review (below) found that the first version changed buildings' colours as their near facades came in: office spandrels and mullions in a grey cladding covered about a third of the wall, so a cream or green office turned blue-grey within 140 m (`kawran-metro`, `d05-bus-stop`, `m2-divider`); institutional frames and modern fins had the same fault in a smaller way. Pieces that cover much of a wall (spandrels, mullions, pilasters, columns, beams, fins, bands, cornices, sunshades) now take the wall's own material, D-06b's skin; only small pieces (sills, rails, grilles, signs, slab edges, glass) have colours of their own. Measured on a lone building of each style (`BuildingsKeepTheirColourAsTheirNearFacadesFadeIn`), pieces in other colours cover, as a share of the wall: Office 0.0 %, Institutional 1.3 %, Industrial 1.6 %, Market 2.2 %, WalkUp 2.9 %, Modern 6.9 %; the test holds them to 8 %. **Old Dhaka 27.4 %**: its open shutter leaves in painted wood are the style's mark and the far tier does not paint them, so Old Dhaka buildings do take on their shutters' colours between 140 and 110 m (K-48; not seen in the player, which streams only the slice).

## Not done here, and why

- **Old Dhaka and Modern are not visible in the player.** The player streams only the slice (Kawran Bazar to Farmgate), which holds neither; both styles are built and tested in EditMode on c_8_-21 (Old Dhaka) and c_13_-35 (Gulshan), not seen in a Unity shot.
- **Level 1 landmarks**: the 271-row candidate list awaits the owner; no geometry. Names include hotels, hospitals and companies: no name, logo or livery appears without authorization.
- **Inferred styles and districts**: every style is a rule from category, type and the nearest named place; no facade is mapped and no reference was used.
- **Weathering** (roadmap's D-06 row) is still the shader's macro wear and repair patches (D-02); the near pieces carry no stains or streaks of their own.
- **Not drawn**: window air-conditioning units, external pipes and meters, laundry, planters, door frames and entrance canopies of homes, the inner rings of courtyards, walls cut along the chunk border, upper pieces of buildings on stilts or decks (only buildings on the ground).
- **Signboards stay blank** until Bengali signs are designed (K-41).
