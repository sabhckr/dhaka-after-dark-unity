# Environment roadmap (Phase D)

Plan for the owner's "Environment & visual production master directive" (2026-11-02). It turns the data-accurate Farmgate slice into a detailed, believable contemporary Dhaka. The look is gritty and urban, but every asset is original. Phase D builds it on the Farmgate box first; then it scales through the chunk pipeline.

This plan replaces the one-line Phase D summary in [FEATURE_BACKLOG.md](FEATURE_BACKLOG.md). Estimates are in focused working days and are re-set at each milestone from the measured pace.

**Rendering today**

- Ground, roads and buildings are unlit vertex colour. Their meshes have positions and colours only: no normals, no UVs, no textures.
- URP Forward+, HDR, SSAO, main-light shadows to 50 m, MSAA off.
- One directional light and a default sky; no time of day.

**What is not in the data and must be inferred:**

- tree species
- electric poles and overhead wires
- most sidewalks, kerbs and road markings
- facade families, roofs and materials
- shops and their names

Inferred content is deterministic (seeded by OSM ids), documented per rule, and visible as "inferred" in a debug view. It never overwrites the canonical data.

**Text:** TextMeshPro does not shape complex scripts. Bengali conjuncts and vowel signs need reordering and ligatures, which it does not do. So signs cannot be typeset at run time with it.

## Decisions

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ED-1 | One world shader, `Dad/World`: URP PBR lighting (`UniversalFragmentPBR`), materials from texture arrays chosen per vertex (the id rides in the vertex colour's alpha), UVs in world metres: plan view on flat faces, box-projected on walls. Chunk meshes keep no normals: the shader takes the face normal from screen-space derivatives. Trees and props have their own normals. Facade UVs that line up with building corners come from the builder in D-06 | Ground, roads and buildings are flat-faceted, so the derivative normal is exact per triangle (to about 1e-3). It adds nothing to the mesh or the apply step. A UV direction taken from that normal was not precise enough: dotted with positions kilometres from the origin, its noise became metres (seen in D-01's metre-grid view), hence box projection on walls. If the normal fails visually or in SSAO, the fallback is packed 8-bit normals |
| ED-2 | Textures are generated by a seeded editor tool. Tileable PBR sets: albedo, normal, and a mask with roughness, AO and wear. Clean, aged, dirty, cracked, wet, repaired and faded come from masks and parameters, not separate textures. Tiling is broken by macro variation and stochastic sampling | Original, licence-free and reproducible. CC0 photo sets (Poly Haven, ambientCG) are a later upgrade, only with the owner's approval of each download |
| ED-3 | Bengali signs are rasterised by a real shaper at build time: HarfBuzz, through headless Chrome, with OFL fonts. They become texture atlases on shared quads. A test proves shaping is on: strings with conjuncts must render differently once a ZWNJ is put between their letters | Correct conjuncts and vowel signs; cheap at run time; reproducible |
| ED-4 | Trees come from a procedural species generator: trunk, branching, canopy, leaf cards, bark and flower colour per species. They get 3 mesh LODs plus a far card, vertex-colour wind weights, and are drawn instanced per chunk | Original, distinct silhouettes, light on memory. No downloaded tree models |
| ED-5 | Repeated props (tanks, AC units, dishes, poles, lamps, stalls, trees) are drawn instanced per chunk from arrays the workers build, not one GameObject each. Colliders go on one object per chunk | Keeps GameObjects and apply cost flat as detail grows |
| ED-6 | Time of day uses the real solar position for Dhaka (23.8° N, 90.4° E) on an in-game date. Sky, ambient, fog, sun, moon and a global night factor are curves over sun height, not presets | No jumps between presets; physically placed sun |
| ED-7 | Shop names are invented from common Bengali patterns. Each is checked against well-known Bangladeshi brands before it is used, and no real logos are used. The owner rejected হাজী বিরিয়ানি ঘর (2026-11-02) as too close to a real restaurant brand: the biryani house gets an invented name | The directive forbids real trademarks |

## Definition of done (from the directive)

- A coherent Farmgate environment with real materials and architectural variety.
- Correctly shaped Bengali signs.
- Plausible street infrastructure.
- At least 20 distinct tree species, integrated.
- A continuous day and night cycle.
- No visible chunk seams or unexpected popping.
- Performance and memory measured in player builds.
- Regression tests passing.
- A reproducible visual benchmark.
- Updated docs and local commits.

A milestone is not done from a sample asset or a screenshot alone.

## Risks

- **An 8 GB M1 is the only test machine.** GPU figures are M1 figures; Windows tiers stay unmeasured (K-02).
- **Procedural art quality.** Generated textures and trees will not match photo-scanned assets. Golden views show the real result. CC0 photo sets would be a separate, approved step.
- **Detail versus the 4 ms apply budget.** Props and markings add worker time and upload size. Instancing keeps the apply step small, and each milestone re-measures it.
- **Storage.** Generated textures and their imported copies are budgeted per milestone, within the 7.4 GB headroom.
